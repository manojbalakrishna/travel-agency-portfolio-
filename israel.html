FAQ

Common questions

How many days do I need for Israel?

Most pilgrims choose 7 to 8 days. We can make it shorter or add Jordan or Egypt.

Do I need a visa for Israel?

Most Indian passport holders need a visa. Our visa assistance service checks your documents and helps with the application. The embassy makes the final decision.

Can you arrange flights?

Yes. See our air ticket service.

Is the trip safe?

We check current travel advisories before we confirm dates, and we tell you early if a plan needs to change.

How much does the trip cost?

Price depends on dates, hotel level and group size. Send an enquiry and we reply with a written quote before you commit.
