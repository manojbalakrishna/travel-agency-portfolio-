FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Ask us in the form below.

What does a tour package include?

Each package lists its stay, meals, transfers and guided activities. Flights are booked separately unless the package says otherwise.

How do I book a package?

Open a package, tap Book this package and send your details. We confirm hotels and dates with you by email before you pay.

Can I change a package to suit me?

Yes. Tell us your dates, budget and must-see places in the contact form and we will adjust the plan.

When do I pay, and what if plans change?

You pay only after we confirm your trip. Change and cancellation terms are shared with the quote, before you commit.

Do you help with visas and documents?

Yes. Our visa assistance service checks your documents, fills the forms and books your appointment. The embassy always makes the final decision.

Do you offer group or family pricing?

Yes. Send us the number of travelers and we will quote a group or family rate.

Do you organise Christian pilgrimages?

Yes. We run trips to the Holy Land, Rome and local shrines such as Velankanni, with a pilgrim guide. Church and family groups get special group rates.

Do you run student and college tours?

Yes. Student trips come with shared stays, a trip coordinator and student pricing. Ask us about group rates for colleges and schools.

Can I book only tickets, without a package?

Yes. Request flight, train or bus tickets from the Ticket booking service and we will send the best options and fares.
